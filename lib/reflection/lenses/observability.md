# Lens: observability

**Use when** the result will run without anyone watching it.

## Questions

1. How will we know it works after it is delivered, and how soon?
2. How will we know it stopped working, and who is told?
3. When it misbehaves, what evidence exists to find out why?
4. Does that evidence avoid exposing anything sensitive?
5. What does normal look like, so abnormal can be seen?
6. What is the cost of recording it, and is it worth it?

## Stop when

There is a concrete way to detect both success and breakage without reading the code.
