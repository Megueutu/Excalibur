# Lens: user impact

**Use when** a person will see, wait for, or depend on the result, including when it goes wrong.

## Questions

1. Who is affected, and what do they notice first?
2. What do they have to do differently after this change?
3. What do they see when it fails, and can they recover without help?
4. Is anything they relied on being taken away or changed silently?
5. Does the wording make sense to someone who does not know how it works?
6. What is the smallest version that already helps them?

## Stop when

The affected people, their first experience and their failure experience are described in their own terms.
