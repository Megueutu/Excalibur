# Lens: simplicity

**Use when** the plan feels large, or when options keep being added.

## Questions

1. What is the smallest change that solves the stated problem?
2. What am I building for a need nobody has stated?
3. What existing piece already does most of this?
4. What can be removed without anyone noticing?
5. Will the next reader understand this without being told the history?
6. Is the complexity here essential, or a leftover of an earlier approach?

## Stop when

Everything left in the plan traces back to the stated problem.
