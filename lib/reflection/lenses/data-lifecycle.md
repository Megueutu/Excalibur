# Lens: data lifecycle

**Use when** information is created, stored, moved, changed or removed.

## Questions

1. Where does each piece of information come from, and who owns it?
2. What shape must it keep for readers that already exist?
3. How does it change over time, and what happens to what was stored under the old shape?
4. How long must it live, and what removes it afterwards?
5. Is any of it personal, sensitive or costly to recreate?
6. Is there one source of truth, or are there copies that can drift?

## Stop when

Ownership, shape changes and removal are each answered for every piece of data involved.
